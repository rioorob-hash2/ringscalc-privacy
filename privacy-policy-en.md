# Privacy Policy — "Ring Weight" (RingsCalc)

Effective date: 2026-11-08

The "Ring Weight" app (package `com.ringscalc.app`) does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

## Data collected
None. The app requests no internet access, contains no ads or analytics, and requires no registration.

## Local data
All calculations run on the device. Settings (language, theme) are stored locally and never leave the device. Saved PNG images are created by explicit user action and remain on the device.

## Permissions
The app requests no user-facing permissions (camera, location, contacts, files, etc.). Saving an image uses the system file-save dialog.

## Third parties
No data is shared: the app works offline and has no network permissions.

## Children
The app collects no data and may be used without age restrictions.
